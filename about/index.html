About

Novi Sad

I'm a software engineer based in Novi Sad, Serbia, a dynamic city on the banks of the Danube that blends history, culture, and a growing tech scene. My work focuses on building robust, well-designed software systems, where clean architecture and thoughtful user experience go hand in hand. Outside of day-to-day engineering, I value balance and inspiration. I enjoy exploring the old town, discovering local cuisine, and spending time in environments that encourage curiosity, creativity, and meaningful collaboration.
